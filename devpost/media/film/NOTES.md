# Film stills (Lens Studio 5.23.2 preview, SPECS 27 device, 1505 x 1590 px)

Each still is the preview panel's own screenshot (PreviewPanelTool screenshot) with Lens Studio maximized and LEAF's on-screen log camera switched off by the media helper (`hideLeafOverlay`). The preview camera is reset (MovePreviewCamera reset) before each helper runs; a pitch in the note is a MovePreviewCamera rotate from that reset pose. Sample lamp values, computed reflection: the Lens labels them so on screen.

All seven stills are v0.4 (BUILD_LOG row 21), retaken on 2026-11-02 with the same helpers and camera moves as v0.3.3, in the "Sunlit Room" preview scene (the v0.3.3 stills used the plain "Plane" scene). Changed since v0.3.3: the answer card is one line plus the verdict (with feet and inches), the floor status line gives the verdict for all three eye heights, and the floor has an eye-height ring per height.

- `01_opening_view.png`: refresh the preview (no helper): the answer card ("Standing: glare up to 32.3 cm (1 ft 1 in) from the wall", "Glare in the viewing area: Seated, Child seated", the disclosure), the print, the two aim handles, the buttons.
