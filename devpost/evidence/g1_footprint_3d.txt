G1: the 3D reflection footprint (plane-mirror test on the floor), 2026-11-03.

What it is (Assets/Scripts/GlareMath.ts, section "G1: the 3D footprint"):
- Each lamp is mirrored through the glass plane itself (mirroredLamp3D). The plane may lean forward
  about the glass's bottom edge (Room.leanDeg). An eye on the floor grid glares where the line
  to that image crosses the lit part of the glass rectangle (glarePoint3D). zoneRows3D gives, for each
  2 cm floor row, the x range of eye positions that see each lamp, so a zone has width as well as
  depth; lobeRows keeps each lamp's rows apart (two lamps, two lobes); combinedZoneRows merges them
  and keeps two pieces where the lobes part.
- The side-view functions (zoneRows, zoneDepth, glarePoint, mirroredLamp) are kept unchanged as the
  reference. The switch is Room.footprint: "3d" uses the new path, "2d" (and the unit tests' default
  room) the reference. In the Lens: GlareZoneController.setFootprint3D(true/false); 3D is on.
- The frame's lean: GlareZoneController.setFrameLeanDeg(0 to 15). The print (and its amber cells)
  turns about the glass's bottom edge; the zones, verdict, setback, glare spots, ghost lamp and the
  light level on the glass use the leaning plane. There is no on-screen control for the lean yet:
  it is set through the controller (LEAF), not by the wearer.

Unit tests (tests/glareMath.test.mjs, 54/54; the 50 earlier tests unchanged and passing):
- Upright glass: 3D equals the reference row for row (within 1e-9 cm) and the hand value
  (200 - e) x d / 100 within 0.01 cm: seated 109 cm at 80.78 cm 73.51; child 104 cm at 62 cm 59.52;
  a 96 cm eye at 62 cm 64.48 (LEAF C22). Glare point and image equal too.
- A forward lean against a side-view mirror worked apart from GlareMath, at 3, 5 and 10 deg, for
  160, 109 and 104 cm (within 0.05 cm). At 5 deg: standing 27.76, seated 52.28, child seated 54.68 cm
  (upright 32.31, 73.51, 77.55). The glare point lies on the leaning plane.
- Two lamps: the lobes are mirror images; each lobe edge is where that lamp's glare point appears
  and disappears; with the 20 deg reflector the lobes part near their far end (two pieces at 67 cm,
  a gap over 5 cm).
- The switch: "2d" ignores a 5 deg lean (73.51); "3d" gives 52.28; verdict and setback follow it.
